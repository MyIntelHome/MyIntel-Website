Consumer labels are "free setup", "help after setup", "home safety visit" and "a plan for your home". Timing of check-ins is agreed with the person; a cadence, service level or unlimited-support promise has not been invented. Professional wording remains on /partners.

One motion sensor is not whole-home visibility. The standard pendant is for manual activation, without automatic fall detection. Each MDsense covers a checked area and may miss falls. VPD2 adds voice help, not a second fall detector. SleepSense requires a compatible bed. Modifications and extra health devices remain separate, with their own costs. Professional response readiness remains disabled.
